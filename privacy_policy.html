Privacy Policy

Effective as of 2026-10-25

The short version

  • Everything you write in Friendpad is stored only on your device.
  • There is no account, no server, no analytics and no ads.
  • The Service Provider never receives your friends, notes or meetups.
  • Deleting a friend or the app deletes that data for good.

This privacy policy applies to the Friendpad app for mobile devices (the "Application"), operated by Burak Deniz (the "Service Provider").

Information Collection and Use

The Application does not collect personal information. You do not create an account, and the Application does not send what you enter to the Service Provider or to anyone else.

What you enter in the Application, such as friends' names, notes, talking points, facts, the people in their lives, interests, tags, meetups, birthdays and life events, together with your settings (theme, chosen mascot, bond style and notification preferences), is stored in a database on your device. It is used only to show it back to you and to schedule your reminders.

The Application does not use analytics, advertising, crash reporting or tracking SDKs, and does not use cookies or similar tracking technologies.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, reminders (meetup briefs, birthdays, life events, fading bonds and a weekly digest) are scheduled locally by your device's operating system. They are not sent from a server, and no push token is created or shared. You can turn each kind of reminder off in the Application's settings, or turn notifications off in your device settings.

Contacting Support

If you choose "Email support" in the Application, your email app opens with a draft addressed to the Service Provider. The draft includes the app version, your device model and operating system version to help with troubleshooting. Nothing is sent until you send the email yourself. The Service Provider uses your email and its contents only to answer you, and deletes support emails when they are no longer needed.

Third-Party Services

The Application is distributed through the Apple App Store and Google Play. When the Application asks you to rate it, the rating prompt is shown by Apple or Google, and the Application does not learn whether or how you rated it. These platforms handle data under their own privacy policies:

  • Apple Privacy Policy
  • Google Privacy Policy

Information About Other People

The notes you keep are about your friends and the people in their lives. Please write respectfully and only what you would be comfortable with them seeing, and keep your device locked. This information stays on your device and is never shared by the Application.

Your Rights

Because the Service Provider does not hold your data, you are in full control of it on your device. You can view and edit everything in the Application, delete a friend to remove everything about them, or delete the Application to remove all of its data. If you have contacted support and want those emails deleted, or have any question about your data, contact the Service Provider at meliksahcakir.apps@gmail.com.

If you are a California resident (CCPA/CPRA) or in the European Economic Area or United Kingdom (GDPR), you have rights to access, correct and delete personal data and to object to processing. The Service Provider does not sell or share personal information.

Data Retention and Deletion

Data in the Application stays on your device until you delete it. Your device's own backups (such as iCloud or Google device backups, if you have turned them on) may include the Application's data; those backups are managed by Apple or Google under your account, not by the Service Provider.

Children

The Application is not intended for children under 16 years of age. The Service Provider does not knowingly collect any data from children, and does not collect data from anyone through the Application.

Security

Your data is protected by your device's own security. Keeping your device locked with a passcode or biometrics keeps your notes private. Because nothing is sent over the internet, there is no server copy that could be exposed.

Changes

If a future version adds features such as backup or sync that would move data off your device, this policy will be updated before that happens, and the change will be explained in the Application. The updated policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about privacy while using the Application, please contact the Service Provider at meliksahcakir.apps@gmail.com.
